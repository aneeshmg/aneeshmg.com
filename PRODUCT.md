# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), plain CSS, TypeScript; no animation library (CSS, View Transitions). Deployed to Cloudflare Workers Static Assets at aneeshmg.com. No auth, no database, no server code. Confirmed with the user (2026-10-26).

## Purpose

aneeshmg.com is the personal home base of Aneesh Megravalli Gangadhar (Aneesh MG). It shows one person across several real practices, and routes each visitor to the part of the work they came for:

- **Engineer:** software engineer who likes systems, AI and tinkering. Links to GitHub.
- **Trainer:** fitness professional (side practice), certified in NASM, Animal Flow, SFG and yoga; trains Muay Thai and BJJ. Fitness work lives at fitwith.aneeshmg.com.
- **Author:** first book *Optimal Living* (launching around site launch), more in the works.
- **Maker:** handmade soap and skincare (lip balm, moisturizer) under AuraByAneeshMG, at aura.aneeshmg.com.
- **Wanderer:** hiking, travel, reading, learning, occasional writing.

## Users

Everyone equally: tech peers and recruiters, prospective fitness clients, readers of the book, Aura customers, and friends. No single audience outranks the others. A visitor should grasp the range within seconds and find their door without hunting.

## Success

- Within the first viewport a visitor knows who this is and that this one person spans engineering, fitness, writing and making.
- Each practice is reachable in one action.
- The visit feels like meeting a specific, energetic person, not reading a résumé.

## Constraints and assets

- Full name is always "Aneesh Megravalli Gangadhar". "Aneesh MG" is the short form.
- **No photography available for now.** Design must stand without photos and accept them later.
- Existing assets: name pronunciation clips for "Aneesh" and "Gangadhar" (keep the feature), and an "I'm bored" random-site button fed by a GitHub gist (keep).
- Sub-brands keep their own names: FitWithAneeshMG, AuraByAneeshMG.
- No invented claims: no testimonials, client counts, employers or metrics unless Aneesh supplies them.
- Accessibility: keyboard navigable, reduced-motion respected, content complete without JavaScript.

## Brand commitments and anti-goals

The user rejected the first design (oct26v1, a paper-and-serif "almanac") as too bookish/old-fashioned, too quiet/static, and "not enough of me".

Must not feel like: a dark tech/neon cliché, a generic portfolio template (hero + card grid + contact form), playful-gimmicky (interaction getting in the way of content), or corporate/LinkedIn.
